Contact Us

Have a question, found a bug, or want to suggest a new calculator? We'd love to hear from you. Nexus Web Tools is built by a small team and we read every message.

What Can We Help With?

  • Bug reports — If a calculator gives an unexpected result or a page isn't loading correctly, let us know which tool and what inputs you used.
  • Feature requests — Want a new calculator, unit converter, or feature added to an existing tool? Tell us what you need.
  • Partnerships — Interested in affiliate partnerships, content collaborations, or API access? We're open to discussions.
  • General feedback — Suggestions for improvement, design feedback, or just want to say thanks? Send it through.

Before You Contact Us

Many common questions are answered on our FAQ page. If you're wondering whether our calculators are free (yes), whether we store your data (no — all calculations run in your browser), or how accurate the results are, check there first.

For financial calculators, please note that all results are estimates for educational purposes only and do not constitute financial advice. We cannot provide personalised financial, tax, or investment recommendations.
